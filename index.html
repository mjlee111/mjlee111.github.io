About

I'm a robotics engineer at ROBROS and an undergraduate in Intelligent Systems at the School of Robotics, Kwangwoon University.

My work spans embedded systems, real-time control, robotics software, and hardware integration. From low-level firmware and communication interfaces to learning-based robot behavior, I build and integrate the systems that bring robots to life.

I headed the 17th cohort of RO:BIT, Kwangwoon University's undergraduate robotics lab.
